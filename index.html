To Ela — on your birthday

Ela — today is yours. Not because a number on the calendar says so, but because the world feels just a bit kinder when you are in it. You carry a warmth that quiets the small sharp edges of ordinary days. Your laugh is a thing I try to find on purpose, and your calm makes my rest easier.

You are beautiful in a way that isn’t only about what people first notice — it’s in the steady kindness you give, the way you make space for others, and how you show up even when things are small or messy. Those details add up, quietly, until someone like me looks back and realizes they were lucky to be close to them.

I love how you find joy in small things — the shade of your favorite lipstick, the exact way you fold wrapping paper, the silly perfect order you prefer your playlists. These are not trivial to me; they are where I learn you. On this birthday I want to celebrate all of it: your sharp sense of humor, your patience, and the way your presence rearranges the ordinary into something better.

You make the day brighter, not by trying to, but by being yourself. I’m grateful for every shared hour and every late-night message that felt like a private island. I promise to try harder at keeping those islands frequent.

Happy birthday, Ela. Let’s make today gentle and loud in the good parts. Let’s eat the best thing on the menu, pause for the piano, and laugh until it's time for cake. I hope the rest of the year gives you more of the things you love and fewer of the things you don’t.
